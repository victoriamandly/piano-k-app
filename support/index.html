Piano-K Support

Need help with Piano-K? Start with the quick steps below or contact us directly.

Best viewing

Piano-K is designed for landscape use on supported devices. Rotate your iPhone or iPad sideways before playing.

If sound is unavailable

Confirm that the device volume is turned up and Silent Mode is off, then close and reopen Piano-K.

If a control does not respond

Close and reopen the app. If the issue continues, restart the device and try again.

Contact support

Email us with your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened.
