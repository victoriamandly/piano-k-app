Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Piano-K is designed for young children and their families. We believe a children’s learning app should be simple, safe, and private.

Information we collect

Piano-K does not collect, store, or share personal information. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Tracking, analytics, and advertising

Piano-K does not include advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Children’s privacy

Piano-K is intended for preschool-age learners. The app does not ask children to provide a name, email address, location, photograph, or any other personal information.

Local app content

Piano-K’s songs, artwork, and audio are bundled with the app and operate locally on the device. The app does not upload a child’s playing activity or progress to an external service.

Changes to this policy

If Piano-K’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be sent through our Contact link.
